# Consent and privacy guidance — template

Explain purpose, approximate duration (20–30 minutes), voluntary participation, ability to stop and how notes will be used in the cooperative project. Follow company and university requirements; this is guidance, not invented ethics approval or legal advice. Obtain actual consent before collecting data.

Use synthetic logs only. Do not record names, employee IDs, credentials, customer/device identifiers or live infrastructure. Use a participant code in metrics, with any code-to-name mapping and consent stored privately outside Git. Ask separate permission for screen/audio recording; default to no recording. Define retention, access, deletion date and approved aggregate publication with the supervisor before collection. Never commit signed consent, raw identifiable responses or unredacted recordings.

Participant can skip a task or withdraw. Publish only approved anonymized aggregate themes; do not quote a recognizable statement without permission. CSV is an empty template, not evidence that consent or a session occurred.
